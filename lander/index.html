Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Do I need a minimum number of followers?

No. Reco's supplied official messaging says there is no follower minimum to get started.

Do earnings only come from purchases?

No. Reco states that views, saves and clicks also count as engagement.

Are any earnings amounts guaranteed?

This page does not promise a fixed income. Eligibility, earning calculations and payments depend on the current provider terms.
